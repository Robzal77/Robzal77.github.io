About me

I manage the portfolio. I also understand the platform underneath it.

From implementation engineering to enterprise product leadership.

I started in technology implementation and automation, moved into enterprise BI engineering, then into analytics product and program leadership. That progression is the reason I can operate comfortably across business outcomes and technical systems.

Today I define global analytics roadmaps, lead teams, work with stakeholders across functions and geographies, and guide solutions built with Power BI, SQL, Fabric, Databricks, ADF and Power Platform.
